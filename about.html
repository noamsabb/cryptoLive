CryptoLive

CryptoLive is a single-page web app that brings the world of cryptocurrency right to your browser.

Real-Time Market Display

Browse a complete list of all cryptocurrencies, each showing its logo, symbol, name and live prices in USD, EUR and ILS.

Instant Search

Type any part of a coin’s name or symbol—like “bit”—and instantly see all matching results, no page reloads required.

Select & Compare

Toggle up to five coins for comparison. If you try to select more, a friendly dialog will guide you to remove one before adding another.

Live-Updating Graph

View a dynamic line chart of your chosen coins’ USD values, auto-refreshing every second so you never miss a market movement.
